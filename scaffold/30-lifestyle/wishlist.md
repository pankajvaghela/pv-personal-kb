# Wishlist

Things I might want, held until I have wanted them long enough to trust it.

One line per wish:

    - [ ] <what> — ~$<cost> — added <YYYY-MM-DD> — <why> #wish/buy

`#wish/buy` · `#wish/do` · `#wish/go` · `#wish/learn`. Cost is rough on purpose — it sets how long to wait, not a budget.

A wish leaves this file three ways: **bought**, **promoted** to a goal, project, or trip, or **dropped**. Never by being ignored. An item that has sat off hold for months is not a pending purchase, it is a decision being avoided, and `/pkb-wishlist review` will say so.
